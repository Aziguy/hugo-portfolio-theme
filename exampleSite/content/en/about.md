I am **Alex Martin**, a full-stack developer and quality engineer based in Lyon.

I started by building websites for local businesses, then joined product teams where I discovered
the joy of shipping often without breaking things. Since then I split my time between development and
quality tooling: end-to-end tests, continuous integration and code review.

## How I work

- Understand the need before writing code.
- Ship in small steps, measure, adjust.
- Document what cannot be guessed.

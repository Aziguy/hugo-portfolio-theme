## The context

Every team eventually hits this problem: what worked on a small project no longer holds at scale.

## What works

1. Start small and measure.
2. Automate what repeats.
3. Keep feedback visible to the whole team.

{{< notice type="info" >}}
These tips apply to a two-person team as much as to a whole organisation.
{{< /notice >}}

## In short

Consistency beats tooling: pick a practice, stick to it, then improve it.
